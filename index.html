§01 — about

DI

I am a Lecturer at the University Politehnica of Bucharest and a machine-learning researcher working on dictionary learning and sparse representations, extended to kernel learning, anomaly detection, and classification. I earned my PhD in 2024 from the Doctoral School of Automatic Control and Computers at POLITEHNICA Bucharest.

My most cited contributions introduce new dictionary structures — Cone atoms, Gaussian atoms, and incoherent frames built with distance barriers — published in Signal Processing, IEEE Access, and the IEEE Open Journal of Signal Processing, and presented at ICASSP and EUSIPCO. I turned this research into the open-source Python package dictlearn.

Alongside academia I have worked in industry as a Data Scientist (CrowdStrike, Riverbed) and software engineer (Bitdefender), and I have contributed to applied ML in healthcare and to open-source Romanian large language models.
